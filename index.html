Good to Know

Questions, answered.

How do I book?

Two ways, both first-class: pick a trip and an open date on the booking calendar, or call or text me at (517) 388-6514. A $150 deposit reserves the date, and cancellations require 48 hours notice.

What if it's my first time fishing?

Not a problem. I have the right section of river and the right equipment for beginners — you will have real opportunities at quality fish while learning the skill throughout the day or evening. Kids and family trips are welcome too, and a summer panfish trip on one of the quiet lakes near Traverse City is an easy place to start.

What do I need to bring?

A valid Michigan fishing license (get one on the Michigan DNR website — this is your responsibility), polarized sunglasses for daytime trips, and safety glasses for night trips. Layered clothing and bug spray are recommended. Waders or muck boots and rain gear are suggested depending on the weather. Everything else — rods, reels, flies, leaders, tackle — is on me.

Where are you located?

South Boardman, Michigan, about 20 miles east of Traverse City — within an hour of most major trout streams in Northern Michigan. Whether you are staying in Traverse City, Charlevoix, Petoskey or Manistee, I have a fishing option for you.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

A $150 deposit reserves your date on any trip. All cancellations require 48 hours notice. Gratuity is not included in trip rates.

Do I need my own gear?

No. Rods, reels, flies, leaders and terminal tackle are included on every trip — mice on night trips, and bait, tackle and electronics on ice trips. Spin gear is available if you would rather not fly fish.

How many people can come?

River, panfish and perch trips are priced for up to two anglers; a third angler is $100 more. Ice fishing is priced per angler.
