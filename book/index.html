Booking FAQ

Before you pick a date.

How do I book?

Right here: pick a trip and an open date on the calendar above, and I will confirm by text. Or call or text me at (517) 388-6514 — phone booking is not the fallback, it is the way most of my trips get booked. A $150 deposit reserves the date, and cancellations require 48 hours notice.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

A $150 deposit reserves your date on any trip. All cancellations require 48 hours notice. Gratuity is not included in trip rates.

What if the date I want is not on the calendar?

Call or text me at (517) 388-6514. The calendar shows confirmed open dates, but the river does not always keep to it — if you have a week in mind and nothing is showing, I can often still make it work.

How many people can come?

River, panfish and perch trips are priced for up to two anglers; a third angler is $100 more. Ice fishing is priced per angler.
