Family Trips FAQ

Before you bring the kids.

How do I book?

Two ways, both first-class: pick a trip and an open date on the booking calendar, or call or text me at (517) 388-6514. A $150 deposit reserves the date, and cancellations require 48 hours notice.

Is a panfish trip good for kids?

Yes. Bluegill and panfish are willing, the lakes are calm, the gear is simple, and nobody has to learn to cast to catch fish. Family trips and kids are more than welcome — tell me their ages when you book and I will set the day up around them.

When is panfish season?

Summer, broadly — bluegill and other panfish are on the inland lakes near Traverse City from late spring through the warm months. Fall perch takes over in September, and ice fishing runs from first ice through March.

How young is too young?

There is no hard rule. Tell me their ages when you book and I will pick the water and the length of the day around them — a half day is usually the right call for the youngest anglers. Life jackets are on the boat.

Do we need to bring anything?

A valid Michigan fishing license for anyone 17 or older, and sunscreen. Rods, bait and tackle are included — Michigan does not require a license for anglers under 17.

Do you clean the fish?

Yes, if you want me to. Fish cleaning is not included in the trip rate — it is available for $20 per limit.
